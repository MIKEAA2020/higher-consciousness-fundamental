## 0. What the inversion actually is

The formalization program borrows physicalist machinery — Markov blankets, variational free energy, Bayesian mechanics ([63] L3153), and the representation functor R:𝓜→𝓔 (L3191) — and then performs what [64] itself calls "a radical ontological inversion on Karl Friston's Free Energy Principle" (L3247):

- **Standard physicalist reading:** biological systems minimize variational free energy to resist thermodynamic entropy (L3248).
- **Inverted reading:** "Under analytic idealism, there is no fundamental thermodynamic entropy. There is only Phenomenal Free Energy" (L3249). Ψ minimizes *experiential* prediction error to maintain "stable, localized structures of meaning" (L3251–3253); the Markov blanket is "not a physical membrane" but the Dissociative Boundary (L3254–3257); decombination happens because the undifferentiated field is "computationally and experientially overwhelming" (L3258). Slogan: "Dissociation is the universe's optimal inference strategy" (L3259).

Note what the inversion is **not**: it does not change the sign of the objective — the inverted Ψ still *minimizes* (L3253). It swaps what free energy is *about*. An ontological reinterpretation, not a new dynamical law.

## 1. Critique — where it bites

### Bite 1 — The keystone is named but never written

The entire final act contains exactly one free-energy functional, at L3180 — written in the *pre-inversion* sketch, and it is the standard physicalist one (F = E_q[log q − log p] − log p(o)). After the inversion is declared at L3247–3249, no phenomenal functional appears anywhere. The only places the new mathematics could live — Steps 1 and 5 — are self-flagged: "a placeholder that can be refined" (L3170), "Feasibility: Low… Currently, we cannot" (L3219), "long-term metaphysical placeholders" (L3300), feasibility table Low/Low (L3227–3231).

Consequence: if the inversion changes no symbol, no experiment can discriminate the readings at the level of F. The program's empirical content flows entirely through the physicalist mathematics it inherited. "Falsifiable, mathematically grounded" (L3328) is true of Steps 2–4 only because, as written, they are physicalist active-inference neuroscience with idealist commentary attached.

### Bite 2 — Every operational prediction is shared, and the text concedes it twice

- L3203: "Physicalism predicts the same neural changes but interprets them as cause, not image."
- L3280: "Physicalists can also predict 'global integration' under psychedelics… idealism must predict something physicalism fundamentally forbids."

The psychedelic entropy signature (L3184: increased global integration, decreased local segregation) is Carhart-Harris's entropic-brain hypothesis verbatim. The functor R is IIT's intrinsic/extrinsic duality renamed (L3268–3278: 𝓜 = extrinsic third-person view, 𝓔 = intrinsic first-person view of the same causal architecture) — and IIT's own proponents offer it as ontology-neutral, so R inherits that underdetermination rather than breaking it. The functor test (L3420–3439) checks topological isomorphism (matched Betti numbers) — but isomorphism cannot read the *direction* of the arrow, which is the only thing in dispute: analytic idealism, Russellian monism, and structure-sensitive physicalism all predict the match. The headline predictions are shared too: no-classical-AI-consciousness (L3136–3138) with Searle's biological naturalism; the QC hard limit (L3139–3141) with Penrose's objective reduction, which the framework itself files at rendering-engine/Tier-2 level (L2774).

### Bite 3 — The intervention dilemma (Horn 3, now load-bearing)

Step 5 of the blueprint is explicitly interventionist: "We alter the physical boundary (∂A) and observe the mapping R" (L3445–3448), with the administration of psilocybin, LSD, propofol, ketamine, and TMS (L3451–3477). Randomization cashes exactly the epistemic warrant — measurement independence — that the framework's official Bell-superdeterminism revokes (L1045–1048, L1140, L1899: settings and system "holistically correlated with the total state"). If the choice to administer the drug co-arises with the total state (L1143–1144), the perturbation is not independent of the system's hidden variables, and the crucible's inferential logic fails at the level of the framework's own metaphysics.

The scalpel is already in the text: the framework ranks its own official stance Tier 3, "mechanically ugly" (L2782). The demotion simply needs to be made official (§2.4).

## 2. Steelman — the strongest version

### 2.1 The pullback derivation (highest-value move)

Step 5 already contains the pattern: physical law as the pullback of qualia-space topology under R, L_phys = R*(T_𝓔) (L3214–3218). Apply the same pattern to the keystone: define phenomenal surprisal S_phen over 𝓔 (the Step-1 placeholder), then *derive* thermodynamic entropy as its extrinsic image, S_therm = R*(S_phen), and show that Friston's F (L3180) is the shadow of phenomenal free energy at the boundary.

If executed, the inversion stops being an assertion and becomes a theorem, and physicalist FEP becomes the derived special case — Bite 1 dissolves in one move, using a pattern already on the page. If it cannot be executed consistently with known thermodynamics, the inversion is mathematically falsified before any scanner is booked. Either way it acquires risk, which is what "falsifiable" demands and what the inversion currently lacks.

### 2.2 The floor protocol (sharpened decoupling test)

The decoupling test as stated (L3298) rests on a contested premise — that DMT/ketamine states involve *degraded* local computation (ketamine is a metabolic dissociative that increases local activity in several regions). Steelman: stop classifying by pharmacology, classify by measurement. Pre-register R on normal states; define R's floor — the manifold configuration below which the trained encoding model predicts no reportable experience; then select agent/state windows *by measured local degradation below that floor*; collect hyper-vivid structured retrospective reports inside the floor, with confabulation controls (delayed report, content-specific probes). Physicalism wins if vividness tracks local activity; idealism wins only inside the floor. Falsifiable in both directions.

### 2.3 The TMS flagship

The most underrated test in the blueprint. "The encoding model R should predict exactly which facet of the subject's experiential manifold will distort" (L3475–3477) is bidirectional, pre-registerable, and double-blindable — and it carries its own falsification condition (R's predictive failure). Unlike the functor test it does not suffer arrow-direction ambiguity; unlike the decoupling test its premise is not contested. Promote it: pre-register facet-specific predictions from R, perturb DMN nodes double-blind, score against report.

### 2.4 Cut the horn

Demote Bell-superdeterminism from official stance to Level-1 metaphysical register. The framework already ranks it Tier 3 (L2782); making the demotion official dissolves the intervention dilemma (Bite 3) at zero empirical cost — nothing in Steps 2–4 depends on superdeterminism.

## 3. Stress-test verdict

The formalization program survives as a **correlational** research program: Steps 1–4 are executable and will produce real science (encoding models, topological data analysis, perturbation studies) under any metaphysics. It dies as a **discriminating** program unless one of two things happens: the pullback derivation (§2.1) is executed, or one of the crucible protocols (§2.2/§2.3) is won inside physicalism's predicted floor.

The prior round's verdict — real progress, not yet what it claims — is confirmed and sharpened to a precise charge: the mathematics the inversion needs is not exotic and not absent from the program's own vocabulary. The pullback pattern is on the same page (L3214–3218) and was simply never applied to the keystone. The inversion remains the load-bearing beam with no mathematics under it — and the blueprint for the footing is drawn one section above it.
