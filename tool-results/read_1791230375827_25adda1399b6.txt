  3153→CFD is the lifeblood of Hidden Variable theories (like Bohmian Mechanics). These theories desperately want to believe that the electron already knew what its X-spin was, even when you weren't looking, to preserve classical realism.

  3158→4. Interaction Creates Reality (Relational QM)
  3159→This brings us perfectly back to Relational Quantum Mechanics (RQM) and QBism, which we ranked as the best interpretations for the Avatar's experience.

Without the interaction (the measurement), the relationship is not actualized, and the property does not exist on the dashboard.
  3162→Summary: The Ghost in the Statistics
  3163→Counterfactual Definiteness is the assumption that the universe is a finished book, and we are just shining a flashlight on different pages. If you assume the book is finished, then the words on the unlit pages are definitely there (CFD is true).
