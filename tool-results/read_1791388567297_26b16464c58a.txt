   417→6.1 The register, stated
   418→The objective-language directive
   419→Light is made of photons and is real as far as physics is concerned; darkness is the mere absence of
   420→light. Heat is studied in thermodynamics; cold is the mere absence of heat. Likewise evil is

   424→The register has three clauses. The positive clause: what is real is what appears in the objective
   425→inventory — photons and their fields, energy and its conservation, the states of affairs physics and the
   426→special sciences describe. The privative clause: the negative vocabulary — darkness, cold, evil, defect —
   427→does not add items to that inventory; each term names the absence of a positive item, relative to a
   428→standard or an expectation. The objectivity clause: statements in this register describe what exists and

   434→objectively are states of affairs — organisms with compromised function, agents whose volition is

   436→evil is a valuation made from within a perspective, and the register’s requirement is to state the valuation’s
   437→object — the absence of expected function, good, or alignment — rather than to posit a malevolent entity
