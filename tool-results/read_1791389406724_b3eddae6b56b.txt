It is the identity of its essence and its existence. What it is, is to exist. That is what classical metaphysics calls **aseity**.
   296→   296→
   297→   297→**Why this matters:**
   298→   298→
   299→   299→If you say “everything has a cause,” then the first cause also needs a cause, and you get a contradiction or an infinite regress. So you must restrict the principle: everything **dependent** needs a ground. The ultimate is not dependent. It is the unconditioned condition.
   300→   300→
   301→   301→**Is this the only logically acceptable position?**
   302→   302→
   303→   303→Not exactly. Logically, there are other options:
   304→   304→
   305→   305→1. **Brute fact** — the universe just exists, with no explanation.
   306→   306→2. **Infinite regress** — an endless chain of dependent things, with no ultimate ground.
   307→   307→3. **Self-existent ground** — the ultimate has its explanation within itself.
   308→   308→
   309→   309→Options 1 and 2 are not formal contradictions. They are logically possible. But they are not **rationally satisfactory** if you demand a complete explanation. Brute fact is a refusal to explain. Infinite regress explains each member by another, but never explains the whole series. So if you accept the principle of sufficient reason — that everything must have a sufficient reason — then yes, only the self-existent ground is acceptable.
   310→   310→
   311→   311→So your statement is correct if “logically acceptable” means “rationally complete and non-arbitrary.” It is not the only logically possible position, but it is the only one that avoids brute fact and infinite regress.
   312→   312→
   313→   313→**One more clarification:**
   314→   314→
   315→   315→The self-existent ground cannot be composed of parts. If it were composed of parts, it would depend on those parts. So it must be simple. It is not “physical plus something else.” It is not “mathematical plus something else.” It is a single, simple, non-physical, necessary reality whose essence is to exist. That is the prime mover, the uncaused ground, *ipsum esse subsistens*.
   316→   316→
   317→   317→So your overall point stands: physicalism is not self-existent by its nature. Mathematical truth is necessary but causally inert. Neither can be the first cause. You need a concrete, non-physical, self-existent ground. That is the only position that gives a complete topological explanation without brute fact or endless regress.
