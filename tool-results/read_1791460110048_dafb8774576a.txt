   105→   105→Short answer: **No. Nothing in physics or empirical science directly negates the non-dual “only the Absolute” version.** It is not empirically falsifiable.    111→   111→## Why empirical science cannot negate it
   112→   112→
   113→   113→Non-dualism says:
   114→   114→
   115→   115→- Ultimate level: only the Absolute is.
   116→   116→- Conventional level: the world, time, space, physics, individual minds appear.
   117→   117→- Science studies the regularities of the appearance.
   118→   118→- Science is valid at its level. It is not ultimate.
   119→   119→
   120→   120→If that is the structure, then **no empirical finding can contradict the ultimate claim**. Any finding is a finding within the appearance. It tells you how the appearance behaves. It does not tell you whether there is a second reality behind it, because there is no second reality to test.
   121→   121→
   122→   122→This is not a dodge. It is the logical consequence of the framework. To negate non-dualism empirically, you would need a finding that **cannot** be assimilated as an appearance. But any finding can be so assimilated. That makes the framework empirically unfalsifiable. Unfalsifiability is a weakness for a scientific theory. It is not a refutation.

   239→   239→No. Physics and empirical science cannot decisively contradict or reduce the core ultimate frameworks. They are **empirically underdetermined**. The data are compatible with classical theism, non-dualism, brute fact, infinite regress, and grounded circularity—provided each is formulated carefully. Empirical science can only eliminate **specific auxiliary empirical claims** that some versions add, not the core metaphysical frameworks themselves.
   240→   240→
   241→   241→Here is the breakdown.
   242→   242→
   243→   243→---
   244→   244→
   245→   245→## Why empirical science cannot decide between them
   246→   246→
   247→   247→Ultimate frameworks are not claims about particular physical processes. They are claims about the **conditions of possibility** of any physical process. They answer questions like:
   248→   248→
   249→   249→- Why is there anything at all?
   250→   250→- What is the ground of order?
   251→   251→- Is the physical world ultimate or appearance?
   252→   252→- Is consciousness fundamental or derivative?
   253→   253→
   254→   254→Empirical science studies the regularities within the world. It cannot test claims about the ground of the world, because any data it collects are already within the world. So the ultimate frameworks are not in the same domain as empirical test.
   255→   255→
   256→   256→That does not mean they are all equally good. It means the decision between them is **logical and metaphysical**, not empirical.
   257→   257→
   258→   258→---
   259→   259→
   260→   260→## Check each framework against physics and empirical science
   261→   261→
   262→   262→**Classical theism**
   263→   263→- Compatible with Big Bang cosmology, evolution, quantum mechanics, relativity, neuroscience.
   264→   264→- Brain damage affects the mind because the soul is the form of the body, not a separate ghost.
   265→   265→- God is simple and unchanging; creation is not a temporal change in God.
   266→   266→- No empirical contradiction. Core claims are not testable.
   267→   267→
   268→   268→**Non-dualism (only the Absolute)**
   269→   269→- Absorbs all data as conventional appearance.
   270→   270→- Brain damage, psychedelics, split-brain, anesthesia: all changes in the perspective, not in the Absolute.
   271→   271→- Big Bang: a conventional event within time; the Absolute is timeless.
   272→   272→- No empirical contradiction. It is empirically unfalsifiable.
   273→   273→
   274→   274→**Brute fact**
   275→   275→- Says the universe or physical reality just exists with no ultimate reason.
   276→   276→- Fully compatible with all data. It just refuses to explain the ultimate.
   277→   277→- No empirical contradiction. It is a stopping point, not a testable claim.
   278→   278→
   279→   279→**Infinite regress**
   280→   280→- Can be temporal (endless past) or ontological (endless simultaneous dependence).
   281→   281→- Big Bang does not rule out an eternal multiverse, bouncing cosmology, or quantum gravity without a singularity.
   282→   282→- Even if the past is finite, an ontological infinite regress of dependence is not ruled out.
   283→   283→- No empirical contradiction.
   284→   284→
   285→   285→**Circular causality as ultimate**
   286→   286→- Physics has many local circular causal structures: feedback, self-organization, bootstrap, holography.
   287→   287→- No empirical evidence for or against circularity as the ultimate ground.
   288→   288→- Conceptually unstable as ultimate, but not empirically contradicted.
   289→   289→
   290→   290→**Grounded circularity (ground + real circular patterns)**
   291→   291→- Compatible with all known physics. It simply says the ground is not a loop, but loops exist within the grounded order.
   292→   292→- No empirical contradiction.
   293→   293→
   294→   294→---
   295→   295→
   296→   296→## What empirical science *can* eliminate
   297→   297→
   298→   298→Empirical science can contradict **specific auxiliary empirical claims** that some versions of these frameworks add. Examples:
   299→   299→
   300→   300→- Young-earth creationism: contradicted by geology, cosmology, biology.
   301→   301→- Fixed species: contradicted by evolution.
   302→   302→- Mind completely independent of brain: contradicted by neuroscience.
   303→   303→- Perpetual motion: contradicted by thermodynamics.
   304→   304→- A static eternal universe: contradicted by cosmic expansion (though an eternal dynamic universe is not ruled out).
   305→   305→
   306→   306→But these are not the core frameworks. Classical theism does not require young-earth creationism. Non-dualism does not require the brain to be irrelevant. Infinite regress does not require a static universe.
   307→   307→
   308→   308→So empirical science eliminates **bad empirical add-ons**, not the ultimate frameworks themselves.
   309→   309→
   310→   310→---
   311→   311→
   312→   312→## The only real reductions are logical/metaphysical
   313→   313→
   314→   314→If you accept a strong Principle of Sufficient Reason, you can eliminate:
   315→   315→
   316→   316→- Brute fact (leaves ultimate unintelligible).
   317→   317→- Infinite regress (never completes explanation).
   318→   318→- Pure circularity (collapses into brute fact or hidden ground).
   319→   319→
   320→   320→If you reject the PSR, those remain logically possible. That is a metaphysical choice, not an empirical finding.
   321→   321→
   322→   322→If you demand non-duality and no relation, you eliminate classical theism. That is a logical/metaphysical choice, not empirical.
   323→   323→
   324→   324→If you demand a real world and personal meaning, you eliminate radical non-dualism. Again, not empirical.
   325→   325→
   326→   326→---
   327→   327→
   328→   328→## The honest conclusion
   329→   329→
   330→   330→**Physics and empirical science cannot reduce the options.** They are silent on the ultimate. They can only:
   331→   331→
   332→   332→1. Confirm that all major frameworks can accommodate the data.
   333→   333→2. Eliminate specific empirical auxiliaries that some versions add.
